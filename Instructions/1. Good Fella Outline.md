**Doc 1 of 3: Good Fella Site Outline** (source: good-fella.com, audited live)

## 1. Site overview

**Positioning:** "The digital landmark studio." A two-brother custom frontend studio (Julian and Adrian Fella, Good Fella Studio GmbH) building motion-led marketing sites for consumer, sports and entertainment brands. Scarcity driven ("Only 3 spots left", waitlist).

**Stack (detected):** Next.js (React), custom motion (no global GSAP/Lenis/Three objects exposed, so likely bundled or CSS/canvas based), CMS-driven case studies.

**Design tokens**

| Token | Value | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Accent | #FD551D (hover #FB460D) | CTAs, markers, labels, page wipe |
| Ink | #141314 | Dark sections, text on light |
| Paper | #EEEEEE | Light sections |
| Card | #F7F7F7 | Stat and pricing cards |
| Greys | #333333, #696869, #818081 | Body, muted text |
| Display / body font | Aktiv Grotesk (Adobe, paid) | Headlines, body |
| Label font | Geist Mono, uppercase, tracked | Nav, buttons, tags, eyebrows |

**Global UI**

- **Header:** floating inset bar, logo left (blocky "GOOD" wordmark), MENU + hamburger centre, split CTA right ("LET'S WORK TOGETHER" + square "+" button). Bar tints to match the section below.
- **Menu overlay:** large page links (Home, Work, Pricing, About, Contact) with active square marker, contact column (emails, socials, "Working globally", waitlist notes), two image cards (About the studio, Featured project).
- **Button system:** every CTA is a label button + detached square "+" button.
- **Section labels:** right-aligned mono eyebrows in accent, e.g. "// PROCESS", "// THE BUILD".
- **Footer:** newsletter (Name, Email, Subscribe), centred page links, contact emails, Privacy Policy, Legal Notice, keyboard hints (⌘G grid, C change colour), ASCII art animation, "© 2026 Good Fella Studio GmbH. Let the Fellas handle it.", giant cropped wordmark.
- **Floating widget:** small asterisk badge pinned right edge (third-party tool).

- **Contact form:** 6 fields + required budget select (EUR/month). Posts to their backend.
- **Booking:** "Book a 15-min call" external scheduler.

- **Socials:** Instagram, LinkedIn, GitHub.

- **SEO:** unique title per page, meta description, OG and Twitter card tags, canonical.
