```text
ROLE
You are a senior creative frontend engineer (Awwwards level). Build the new marketing site for Streamline Digital Solutions, an AI-native ecommerce growth agency in Cape Town, South Africa.

REFERENCE, NOT SOURCE
The layout and motion patterns are inspired by good-fella.com. Do NOT copy any of its code, copy, images, logos, names or ASCII artwork. Everything must be original. Before finishing, grep the repo for "Good Fella", "Fella", "landmark", "brothers" and remove any hit.

STACK
Next.js 14+ App Router, TypeScript strict, Tailwind CSS, GSAP + ScrollTrigger, Lenis smooth scroll, Canvas 2D (ASCII/dot art), Supabase, Resend, Vercel. No other UI libraries.

DESIGN TOKENS (tailwind.config + CSS vars)

Buttons on teal use ink text. Every text pair must pass WCAG AA.
Copy rules: short declarative headlines ending in a full stop. Never use em dashes.

GLOBAL COMPONENTS
1. Header: floating inset bar, 16px from edges. Logo left (/public/logo.svg, invert on dark), "MENU =" centre, split CTA right: label button "BOOK A GROWTH CALL" + separate square "+" button. Background tints to match the section underneath (use data-theme="dark|light" on sections + ScrollTrigger).
2. MenuOverlay: big links Home, Services, Work, Pricing, About, Contact with teal square marker on active route; contact column; two image cards (About, Featured case).
3. Button: label + detached "+" square, both teal fill.
4. Eyebrow: right-aligned mono label "// SECTION" in accent.
5. SectionHeading: H2 with (a) a teal bar that stretches then collapses into an underline on enter, (b) blinking teal caret after the last letter.
6. ScrambleText: glyphs from "0123456789$%+" resolve left to right, 600ms.
7. Odometer: per-digit vertical roll on enter.
8. RouteTransition: teal diagonal panel wipe (angle from top-left to bottom-right) on navigation.
9. Loader: teal S mark stroke-draws in, max 1.2s, sessionStorage flag to skip on repeat visits.
10. Footer: newsletter (Name, Email, Subscribe), page links, email, socials, Privacy, Terms, "⌘G grid" hint, ASCII data-stream canvas (glyphs drifting left to right), line "Streamline Digital Solutions. Cape Town, South Africa. Built to scale.", giant cropped "Streamline" wordmark.
11. GridOverlay: Cmd/Ctrl+G toggles a 12-col grid overlay.
12. All motion wrapped in a useReducedMotion guard: if reduced, render final states with no animation.

PAGES
/ (home)

CTAs "SEE THE RESULTS" (to /work) + text link "Book a growth call". - Stats (light): photo card + 3 staggered stat cards with Odometer: Revenue driven for clients [CONFIRM], Ad spend managed [CONFIRM], Projects shipped [CONFIRM].

Within two business days you get your three biggest revenue leaks." 02 Strategy call: "One call. You leave with the plan, the timeline and the cost." 03 Build and launch: "Design, dev, tracking and automations shipped together." 04 Scale: "Monthly optimisation sprints across ads, SEO and CRO."
- Selected work (dark): sticky left column (title "Selected work.", thumb index with active marker, VIEW ALL button), right column of large mockups with tags. Data from /content/cases.ts.
- The engine (light): 6 pillars in 3-col grid, ScrambleText titles: Conversion-first design / AI and automation built in / SEO and ads from day one / CRM that closes the loop / Senior team, AI speed / Measured in revenue. One line each, write them.
- FAQ (dark): accordion from /content/faq.ts (6 items: Shopify, who you work with, timelines, only ecommerce?, tech, after launch).
- CTA (light): "Start growing." email, BOOK A 15-MIN GROWTH CALL, photo.

/services: hero "Everything your store needs to scale." then 7 blocks (Web design and development, SEO, Meta and Google Ads, App development, CRM development, Automations, AI solutions), each with outcome line, 3 deliverables, related case link. Close "Need something else? We'll figure it out." + CTA.

/work: intro with 2 odometer stats, filter dropdown by service tag [WEB][SEO][ADS][CRM][AUTOMATION][AI], thumbnail index, 3 view modes (carousel, 2-col, grid), prev/next.

/work/[slug] (generateStaticParams from cases.ts): full-bleed mockup hero, title + View live site, meta row (Timeline, Services, Stack, Market, Year), tagline, The Challenge, The Solution, The Results (3 odometer metrics), CTA, "Next case" scroll-to-advance. Seed cases: vrve, futurama, camping-fridge-sa, wild-clone with [CONFIRM] placeholders for all metrics.

Engagement length table: Launch 4 to 8 weeks, Growth Engine monthly 3-month minimum [CONFIRM], Custom scoped. FAQ incl. "Why no prices on the page?"

DATA + ENV

Work step by step. After each build-order step, summarise what changed and what is next.
```
